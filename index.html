Privacy Policy (English)

Responsible Party

This privacy policy applies to the app Particle Flow (hereinafter "the App"), published by kabel2.

We take the protection of your data seriously. This privacy policy informs you about what data is processed in connection with the use of the App.

2. What Data is Collected?

Particle Flow does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data whatsoever.

The App works completely offline. No information about user behavior, device IDs, or locations is recorded.

Local Settings

Your individual app settings (e.g., particle count, colors, speed) are stored exclusively locally on your device in so-called Shared Preferences. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third parties.

The App requires no internet connection and contains no advertising. No trackers, analytics tools, or advertising SDKs are used.

4. Data Sharing

Since no personal data is collected, there is no sharing of data with third parties.

5. Data Retention

Local settings remain stored on your device until you uninstall the App or clear the App data via your device's system settings.
